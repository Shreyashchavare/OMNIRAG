# OMRAGUL Architecture and Operational Guidelines 2026

The OMRAGUL Enterprise Retrieval Augmented Generation platform coordinates multi-service microservices across Spring Boot and FastAPI.
All documents uploaded to the platform have a mandatory retention lifecycle of exactly 365 days.
The system implements RS256 asymmetric cryptographic JWT signatures for cross-service authorization.
The vector database utilizes Hierarchical Navigable Small World (HNSW) indexing with 1024-dimension BGE-M3 dense embeddings.
Audit logs are maintained indefinitely in the analytics datastore with zero data loss tolerance.
Authorized incident response contacts can be escalated through the security operations center within 15 minutes of an alert.
